Bald Knight Games ("we", "us", or "our") is an independent developer based in Ireland. We respect the privacy of people who use our games and applications.

This Privacy Policy describes how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use our mobile games, applications, and related services (together, our "Apps"). It also explains your privacy choices and rights.

Important: Our Apps offer different features. Not every App uses every service or processes every category of data described below. Processing depends on the App, the features you choose, the permissions you grant, and applicable law.

1. Apps Covered by This Policy

This policy is intended to apply to mobile games and applications published by Bald Knight Games, including KapslFootball, PokerGrid / PokerGrid Rivals, Zombie Mosaic, and other Bald Knight Games applications where this policy is linked in the App or its store listing.

Where an App has an additional or more specific privacy notice, that notice may provide further information about its features.

2. Information We May Process

Device and technical information

  • Device type, operating system, App version, and general technical information.
  • Crash diagnostics, performance data, and information needed to maintain functionality.
  • Device or advertising identifiers, where available and permitted.

Game progress and account-related information

  • Scores, achievements, ranks, game progress, saved teams, and settings.
  • Virtual currencies, owned items, unlocked content, and purchase entitlements.
  • Game-service identifiers, display names, multiplayer sessions, and match information when you choose to use online gaming features.

Content you provide

Depending on the App, you may choose to provide names for teams or players, images and avatars, photos, recipes, shopping lists, pantry information, receipts, documents, prompts, or other content. An App may process this content locally or through a service needed for the feature you requested.

Not every App requires an account. Where account information or a third-party sign-in is used, the information involved depends on the selected service.

3. Advertising, Consent, and App Tracking Transparency

Some free Apps display advertisements through Google AdMob. Depending on the App, region, device settings, and your choices, the advertising provider may process information including advertising identifiers, device characteristics, approximate location inferred from technical signals, App activity, and diagnostics.

On iOS, Apps that require permission for tracking use Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. If you select "Ask App Not to Track", we respect that choice and do not intentionally engage in tracking that requires ATT authorization. Ads may still be shown without cross-app tracking where permitted. Ad data collection that is not considered tracking under Apple's rules may still occur, subject to applicable laws and consent choices.

Where required, an App may use Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to explain advertising data processing and obtain or manage consent. UMP consent and Apple ATT authorization serve different purposes; granting one does not override a denial of the other.

Advertising choices may be available within the App or through device privacy settings. See Google's Privacy Policy for information about Google's processing.

4. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Some Apps offer optional in-app purchases, such as removing advertisements, virtual currencies, digital content, and premium features. Some Apps also offer auto-renewable subscriptions, including Kapsl Club Monthly in KapslFootball, where available.

Transactions are handled through Apple App Store or Google Play, as applicable. We do not directly receive your full payment card details. We may process product identifiers, purchase status, transaction identifiers, and subscription entitlements as needed to deliver and restore purchased features or investigate purchase-related issues.

Subscription pricing, duration, renewal terms, and cancellation details are presented in the relevant App's purchase interface and by the app store. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple or Google account settings, subject to the relevant store's terms.

For Apple subscriptions, see the Apple Standard Terms of Use (EULA).

5. Game Center, Google Play Games, and Multiplayer

Supported games may use Apple Game Center, Google Play Games Services, or other multiplayer systems for authentication, achievements, leaderboards, invitations, and online matches.

Such features may process player identifiers, display names, leaderboard scores, achievements, match results, and information necessary to connect players. Data handled by Apple or Google is also subject to their respective privacy notices.

Some games offer local multiplayer using Bluetooth or local network connections. In such cases, devices may exchange gameplay information required for the connection. Bluetooth multiplayer is not used by us for location tracking.

6. Cloud Storage, Authentication, and Synchronization

Some Apps may use Firebase, Apple iCloud / CloudKit, or other backend services to enable online gameplay, authentication (including anonymous authentication), syncing of saved data, shared lists, cloud storage, or recovery of progress.

Data sent to such services depends on the feature you use. Other Apps may save their progress and preferences only on your device. Data stored locally is not necessarily transmitted to a cloud service.

7. AI-Powered Features

Certain Apps may include optional AI-assisted features, such as recipe suggestions, household assistance, text generation, or analysis of content you submit.

When you actively use an AI feature, the submitted text, images, or other relevant content may be transmitted to a third-party AI service provider so it can produce a response. The applicable provider and handling of information depend on the feature's implementation.

Please avoid providing unnecessary sensitive information in prompts or uploads. We aim to limit information shared with service providers to what is needed to perform the selected function.

8. Device Permissions

Depending on the App, certain features may request permission to access photos, camera, microphone, Bluetooth, local network, notifications, or other device capabilities.

Permissions are used for the features described by the App or the device permission prompt. You can manage permissions in your device settings. Granting access to photos, Bluetooth, or another device feature does not itself authorize advertising tracking.

9. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

App information may be stored locally or, when a cloud or online feature is used, by relevant service providers. We retain or process information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide features, protect our services, handle purchases, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

Uninstalling an App may remove local data, but may not delete information associated with app-store purchases, Game Center, Google Play Games, or cloud accounts. Third-party providers maintain their own retention policies.

If you wish to request access to or deletion of personal information that we control, contact us using the address in Section 17. We may need information sufficient to identify the relevant App or record before processing a request.

10. Purposes and Legal Bases for Processing

Depending on the service and jurisdiction, data may be processed to:

  • Provide gameplay, application functions, multiplayer features, and cloud synchronization.
  • Save progress, restore purchases, and manage subscription entitlements.
  • Display ads in accordance with applicable permissions and consent choices.
  • Diagnose problems, improve performance, and protect the Apps against misuse.
  • Respond to user requests and meet applicable legal obligations.

For individuals covered by the GDPR, relevant legal bases may include performance of a contract, consent (where required), legitimate interests in secure and reliable operation, and compliance with legal obligations. The appropriate basis depends on the specific activity. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw that consent through available controls or by contacting us, without affecting the lawfulness of processing performed before withdrawal.

11. Sharing and Third-Party Providers

We do not sell personal information. Depending on the App's features, information may be processed by providers such as:

  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform for advertising and consent management.
  • Apple App Store, StoreKit, and Game Center for purchases and gaming services.
  • Google Play and Google Play Games Services for purchases and gaming services.
  • Firebase or other backend providers for online, authentication, and cloud features.
  • Apple iCloud / CloudKit for supported synchronization features.
  • AI service providers for optional AI-powered functionality.

These services may have their own terms and privacy policies. Information may also be disclosed if required by law or when reasonably necessary to prevent fraud, abuse, or threats to security.

Useful third-party privacy notices: Google, Apple.

12. Children's Privacy

Our Apps may appeal to different age groups. We do not knowingly seek personal contact details from children where doing so would violate applicable law. Where parental consent or age-appropriate safeguards are legally required, relevant features and processing must comply with those requirements.

Parents or guardians who believe a child has submitted personal information inappropriately may contact us to request a review or deletion, where applicable.

13. International Data Transfers

Some providers operate internationally and may process data outside Ireland, the European Economic Area, or your country of residence. Where applicable law requires it, appropriate safeguards should apply to those transfers, including contractual protections or other legally recognized transfer mechanisms.

14. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability, or to object to certain processing activities. You may also withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis for processing.

To exercise these rights in relation to information we control, email us using the contact address below. We will assess and respond to requests as required by applicable law.

We may update this policy when our Apps, technology, providers, or legal obligations change. The updated text and effective date will be published at this same URL. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or seek consent.
